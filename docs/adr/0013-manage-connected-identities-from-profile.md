# Manage connected identities from profile

Openlink will let users manage connected identities from the profile page, including linking and unlinking configured OAuth providers, while always retaining at least one valid sign-in method. A connected identity is a valid sign-in method only while its verified provider email matches the user's verified email; identities can remain visible after an email change without being usable for sign-in. Valid connected identity avatars may be selected as the user's profile avatar, and if the selected identity is removed or becomes invalid Openlink will choose another available valid connected identity avatar or fall back to initials. This revises the first OAuth increment from ADR 0009, where provider avatars were stored only on connected identities and manual profile management was deferred.

When connecting an identity from Settings, a verified provider email that matches the user's Openlink email also verifies that local email inside the linking transaction, after identity ownership checks succeed. Requiring prior local verification prevented Google and Discord identities from being connected on installations where email verification is optional: the callback returned to Settings without creating an identity. This uses the same provider proof accepted during OAuth sign-in. Unverified or mismatched provider emails cannot verify the local email or create a connected identity.

Email matching for OAuth linking, sign-in, and connected identity validity ignores case and surrounding whitespace. Provider emails are stored trimmed and lowercase; existing Openlink email spelling is preserved. Previously only provider emails were lowercased, so a mixed-case Openlink address could reject both Google and Discord connections as an email mismatch. Distinct email addresses, including aliases, remain distinct.
